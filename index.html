Who we are

Close enough to watch the sun rise over the Pyramids from your own window.

Maria Pyramids House is a boutique guesthouse in Giza, standing just steps away from the Great Pyramids. We believe a great stay starts with genuine hospitality, not just a comfortable room.

From the moment you arrive, our team helps you plan your days in Cairo, arranges private tours to Saqqara, Dahshur, the Egyptian Museum and beyond, and takes care of every small detail so you can simply enjoy Egypt.

Whether you are here for one night or a week, we treat every guest like family, because that is exactly how we see you.
